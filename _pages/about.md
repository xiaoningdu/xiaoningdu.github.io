I am an ARC DECRA Fellow and Senior Lecturer (equivalent to a U.S. Associate Professor) in the [Department of Software Systems and Cybersecurity](https://www.monash.edu/it/ssc), [Faculty of Information Technology, Monash University](https://www.monash.edu/it).
I received my Ph.D. from Nanyang Technological University, supervised by [Prof. Yang Liu](https://personal.ntu.edu.sg/yangliu/), and my Bachelor's degree from Fudan University.


My research focuses on the intersection of software engineering and artificial intelligence. 
I am best known for my contributions to [Devign](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper_files/paper/2019/file/49265d2447bc3bbfe9e76306ce40a31f-Paper.pdf), [BigCodeBench](https://bigcode-bench.github.io/), [DeepStellar](https://dl.acm.org/doi/abs/10.1145/3338906.3338954), and [SimPy](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/3650212.3680347). 
My research has been recognized with ACM SIGSOFT Distinguished Paper Awards at ISSTA 2024 and ICSE 2026, as well as ICLR 2025 Oral. I am also the recipient of [2024 Google Research Scholar Award](https://research.google/programs-and-events/research-scholar-program/recipients/) in Software Engineering.

I regularly serve as a reviewer for flagship software engineering conferences and journals, including ICSE, FSE, ASE, ISSTA, TOSEM, and TSE. I have served as an Associate Editor of TOSEM since July 2025.


I am looking for self-motivated Ph.D. students with strong programming skills and relevant research experience. The position is supported by [full scholarships](https://www.monash.edu/it/research/graduate-research/scholarships-and-support/scholarships-and-applications). Please email me with your CV if you are interested. Monash undergraduate and master's students interested in honours or minor thesis projects should check the [available projects](https://supervisorconnect.it.monash.edu/supervisors/xiaoning-du) and include a copy of their transcript when contacting me.
